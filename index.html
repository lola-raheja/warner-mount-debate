Parent companies and their distributors

Large faint circles represent parent companies. Filled bubbles inside them represent distributors sized by wide-release counts. Standalone distributors appear as independent bubbles.

Bubbles are matched by distributor across years, so existing distributors morph in place as their release counts change, new distributors pop in, and discontinued ones fade out. Parent-company circles track M&A activity — for example, Time Warner's label updates to AOL Time Warner in 2001 while the underlying grouping stays the same.
